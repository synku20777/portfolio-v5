Welcome to my blog! I've been meaning to start writing for a while, and here we are.

## Why a blog?

As a UX designer and frontend developer, I spend most of my day solving problems — whether that's mapping user flows, prototyping interactions, or wiring up components in Astro and React. But I realized I rarely take time to **reflect** on what I've learned.

Writing forces clarity. When I explain a concept here, I understand it better myself.

## What to expect

I plan to write about:

- **UX design** — case study breakdowns, design system thinking, accessibility
- **Frontend development** — Astro, React, CSS patterns, performance
- **Process** — how I approach projects, tools I use, lessons learned

## Let's go

If you're into design, code, or both — stick around. I'll try to keep things practical and honest.
