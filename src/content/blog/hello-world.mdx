Welcome to my blog! I've been meaning to start writing for a while, and here we are. wooo!

## Why a blog?

As a UX designer and frontend developer, I spend most of my day solving stupid problems in complicated, time consuming and expenisve ways — whether that's mapping user flows, prototyping interactions, or wiring up components in Astro and React, but mostly arguing with others or playing dumb. But I realized I rarely take time to **reflect** on what I've learned.

Writing forces clarity. When I explain a concept here, I understand it better myself. Basically treat this thing as my diary that is co-authored by ai to make it more digestible.

## What to expect

I plan to write about:

- **UX design** — case study breakdowns, design system thinking, accessibility
- **Frontend development** — Astro, React, CSS patterns, performance, whatever new trendy framework might come out. Fair warning though. I am stupid. I fuck around I find out. That's all this is going to be. Go read whatever Linus Torvalds writes if you're looking for actually smart people.
- **Process** — how I approach projects, tools I use, lessons learned, the traumas acquired and my feelings about them
- **Complaining** - I will whine about how slow or stupid I feel while saying that my degree was bloody useless :(

## Let's go

If you're into design, code, or both — stick around. I'll try to keep things practical and honest.
