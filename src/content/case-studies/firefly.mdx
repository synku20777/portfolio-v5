    <h2 class="text-2xl md:text-3xl font-bold mb-6 text-neutral-800 dark:text-neutral-100">The Challenge</h2>
    <p class="text-neutral-600 dark:text-neutral-400">Create an ecommerce website that effectively communicates the environmental, economic, and comfort benefits of using Firefly. The site needed to address both potential buyers and existing owners, including product instructions and documentation. The interface had to be simple and user-friendly, reflecting the product's philosophy of ease of use, while also delivering a new logo and visual demonstration of how the product works.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="bg-amber-50 dark:bg-amber-950 p-8 rounded-2xl shadow-sm">
    <h2 class="text-2xl md:text-3xl font-bold mb-6 text-neutral-800 dark:text-neutral-100">The Solution</h2>
    <p class="text-neutral-600 dark:text-neutral-400">A modern, responsive, and minimalist UI design that focused on clarity and intuitive navigation. The design process used Hick's Law to refine navigation and reduce decision time, along with clear, visually distinct calls-to-action. We delivered a new logo design following a flat, 2D monochrome direction, and created an animated infographic to visually demonstrate the product's functionality.</p>
  </div>
</div>
