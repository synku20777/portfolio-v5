    <h2 class="text-2xl md:text-3xl font-bold mb-6 text-neutral-800 dark:text-neutral-100">Problem Background</h2>
    <p class="text-neutral-600 dark:text-neutral-400">
      In the current market situation, finding a reliable digital design bureau as a long-term sustainable partner is extremely difficult. Design bureaus tend to work project-to-project, focusing on short campaigns and bold statements. This creates an unsustainable environment that often leaves many projects and materials unfinished after the contract period ends. Additionally, businesses don't always understand the benefits of long-term sustainable design and digital practices, despite the potentially high cost of short-term thinking in today's era.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div class="bg-neutral-50 dark:bg-neutral-950 p-8 rounded-2xl shadow-sm">
    <h2 class="text-2xl md:text-3xl font-bold mb-6 text-neutral-800 dark:text-neutral-100">Our Solution</h2>
    <p class="text-neutral-600 dark:text-neutral-400">
      Creating a design bureau brand called Astra that stands out through its commitment to long-term partnerships and sustainable design practices. By focusing on building ongoing relationships with clients rather than one-off projects, Astra aims to deliver more value and consistent results. The brand combines mid-century and modern futuristic design elements inspired by space themes, projecting both ambition and sophistication.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

      <p class="text-neutral-600 dark:text-neutral-400 mb-6">
        The Astra brand embodies a fusion of mid-century and modern futuristic design, drawing inspiration from space themes (specifically the NASA Graphics Standards Manual), early 20th century avant-garde design, and the work of construction bureaus.
      </p>
      <p class="text-neutral-600 dark:text-neutral-400">
        This fusion is realized through the adoption of design philosophies and techniques
        reminiscent of that era, including the choice of iconic grotesque fonts from the 20th
        century, such as Helvetica and Futura PT.
      </p>
    </div>

    <div>
      <h3 class="text-lg font-bold mb-4 text-neutral-800 dark:text-neutral-100">Brand Elements</h3>
      <div class="space-y-6">

          <div class="text-sm font-medium text-neutral-500 dark:text-neutral-400 mb-2">Name &amp; Concept</div>
          <p class="text-neutral-700 dark:text-neutral-300">
            The brand's identity is defined by its nomenclature, where the choice of "bureau" over "studio" evokes a sense of structure, exclusivity, and distinction, underscoring its alignment with the ethos of industrial and engineering design from historical construction bureaus known for their extraordinary products and designs. The cosmic and sophisticated theme continues to be a cornerstone of the brand's
            identity, encapsulated in its Latin motto and name, which underscore its ambition and
            sophistication within the cosmic theme.
          </p>
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>
